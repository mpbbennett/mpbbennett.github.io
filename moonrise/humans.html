Names and Naming

Humans

Humans are the most numerous people in Moonrise, though not an absolute majority. They can interbreed with all the other peoples, sharing characteristics from each parent.

Humans are found in almost every major faction and region. They are the dominant population of the Old Dominion, The League, and Triland, and are present in significant numbers throughout the continent.

The Dreaming Coast is noted for its cannabis use and Chinese-influenced cuisine. The Old Dominion favours opium and Eastern European food. The Lakelands run on coffee and coca, with Indian cuisine.

Languages

All human languages belong to the Common language family and are mutually intelligible, though regional dialects are distinctive:

  • Common (Standard) — spoken in the League, with American-sounding accents and Dutch naming conventions (e.g. “Corrin Scryer”)
  • Common (Arrowmoor) — southern English accent, Old English names
  • Common (Lakelandic) — French accent and naming (e.g. “Corrin of Rivermead”)
  • Common (Central) — Welsh accent and names
  • Common (Northern) — Spanish accent and names, spoken by humans in wood elf domains
  • Common (Bay) — Scottish accent and names, spoken around the Bay of Song
  • Old Common — spoken in the Old Dominion, posh and old-fashioned, with Old English names in the form “X, Lord of Location”

Half-Elves

Half-elves are common wherever humans and elves live in close proximity. They speak High Elven and any other language, and tend to be versatile and adaptable.

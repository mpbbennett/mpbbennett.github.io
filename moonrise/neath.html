Neath

Neath is a city built under a great shell, and the seat of the League’s Diet.

The Shell

The shell is officially said to be that of a turtle, making the site sacred to the Wise One among the Old Gods. However, anyone can see it was not a turtle — the shape is clearly more crab-like. In truth, the shell belonged to a gigantic aberration from deep antiquity.

Smaller shells around the outside of the main shell are also populated, along with some conventional buildings. Apertures have been carved into the roof of the shell (though not many — it is very thick), and towers have been built upwards through them.

The name “Neath” was originally short for “beneath the shell.”

The Diet

The local ruler leased their palace to the Diet, which chose the location for its symbolic associations with the Wise One’s turtle. The city is actually rather miserable — always dark and somewhat smelly under the shell. The Diet regularly grumbles about this, but the ruler was happy enough to move to a countryside palace.

Dwarves and Goblins love Neath, finding the underground environment familiar and comfortable.

Names and Naming

Calendar

Moonrise uses two overlapping calendars: a lunar calendar for everyday timekeeping and a solar calendar for agricultural and seasonal purposes.

Lunar Calendar

The lunar calendar has 12 months of 28 days each (336-day year). This runs 12 days fast compared to the 348-day solar year.

The months are named after the gods. The first seven are named after the Sacred Seven, in an order explained by a folk tale (see below). The remaining five are named after other gods, including the three no longer widely worshipped:

  1. Swiftmoon (The Swift)
  2. Luckmoon (The Lucky)
  3. Clevermoon (The Clever)
  4. Fairmoon (The Fair)
  5. Heartymoon (The Hearty)
  6. Strongmoon (The Strong)
  7. Wisemoon (The Wise)
  8. Sorrowmoon (The Sorrowful)
    9–10. Two further months, likely named after the Mirthful and the Kind
  9. Hardmoon
  10. (Name not recorded)

Every 7 years there is a leap-day at the end of Sorrowmoon, a day of ill-omen.

The Folk Tale of the Race

The order of the first seven months is explained by a folk tale. The old year told the gods that the new year was staging a race across the Godspire Mountains; the moons would be given to the gods in the order they arrived. (The remaining five months, named after lesser or forgotten gods, come after the seven who raced.)

  • Ny the Swift flew there instantly. They arrived first.
  • Lutow the Lucky thought about walking and it made them tired. They took a nap and dreamed they had already arrived. When they awoke, they had.
  • Cirith the Clever decided to build a machine. It took a long time, but the chariot went fast once built.
  • Feelatha the Fair started flying behind the Swift, but every time it rained they sought shelter to avoid getting their beautiful plumes wet. It rained for twenty-eight days straight.
  • Haylva the Hearty began slowly plodding across the mountains. Storms and avalanches struck, but they kept going.
  • Skraddor the Strong did not want to go over the mountains. They thought digging a tunnel underneath would be easier. It was not.
  • Woechen the Wise arrived last, long after all the others. The Swift laughed. “Well, Wise One, did you get lost along the way?” “No, I knew the way. I knew I would not be the one to win the race, so I decided instead that I would be the one to learn most from the journey.”

Phases and Dating

Each month is subdivided into four phases of the primary moon (7 days each): Waxing, Bright, Waning, and Dark. The phase days themselves are generally days of rest. Other days are counted by their proximity to the next phase day.

A secondary moon follows a 42-day cycle. When both moons are Bright simultaneously (every Bright of Swiftmoon, and corresponding points thereafter), these are important feast days.

Dating Convention

Dates are written as: “The 3rd day before the Waxing of Heartymoon in the fifth year of the reign of King X (202AC)” — sometimes abbreviated as “3b wx/5/202”.

“AC” stands for “After the Compact” — the formation of The League serves as the era’s epoch.

Solar Calendar

The solar calendar has a 348-day year: 12 months of 28 days, plus 12 intercalary days distributed as four seasonal festivals:

  • Spring Equinox — 3 days
  • Midsummer — 3 days
  • Autumn Equinox — 3 days
  • Midwinter — 3 days

The solar calendar is used primarily for agriculture and seasonal planning, while the lunar calendar governs religious observance, politics, and daily life.

Campaign Summary

A narrative summary of the Moonrise campaign, played over approximately 52 sessions in Dungeon World. This page covers the main arcs rather than individual session recaps.

Arc 1: Arrowmoor (~Sessions 1-18)

The party formed in Birchfield, a village in the independent duchy of Arrowmoor. Their early adventures included rescuing villagers from slavers using the Delian Tomb as a hideout (discovering a sealed ancient vault beneath it), and dealing with local threats including a giant snake and bandits.

The party gradually became drawn into the political tensions of the duchy. The Combine and the Old Dominion were engaged in a cold war for influence over Arrowmoor, with the ageing Duchess Ethel aligned with the Old Dominion and Count Bertrand of Merrykeep secretly backed by the Combine. The Court Magician Modwen became a key ally, suspecting foul play.

Meanwhile, the party encountered the fringes of a dragon’s blood smuggling network connected to the criminal Anatomist (Hayworth) and the Kin.

Arc 2: Factions and Lycanthropy (~Sessions 19-35)

The campaign expanded as the party took on quests for multiple factions. Caesar infiltrated the Kin, meeting The Carver in Arrowmoor and proving loyalty by murdering Captain Koen. Storr travelled to the Scalewind Isles to find his lost family, striking a deal with the dragon Blazejaw and learning his kin had been petrified by a Medusa.

A pivotal moment came when Magnus and Caesar were both turned into lycanthropes (werebears) — Magnus during an encounter with an uncontrolled werewolf named Reginald, and Caesar when Magnus converted her in a desperate moment. This drew the party into conflict with Inquisitor Selene, who was persecuting lycanthropes, and into contact with werewolf circles in Graveport and Inchcliff.

The party also carried out a heist at a collector’s party, seeking a book for Aldara’s quest to find the Tomb of Archmage Arianell. Caesar killed The Carver and took over his operations.

Arc 3: The Tomb and the Lich (~Sessions 29-40)

Aldara finally found and entered the Tomb of Archmage Arianell, a figure from the era of the Arcane Order. The tomb contained puzzles (elemental obelisks, goblin records, and a mysterious silver cube) and treasures. But Arianell himself was raised — a Lich who tasked the party with retrieving five artifacts from the ruins of The Cloud-Capital:

  1. A record of goblin civilisation after Arianell’s death
  2. A mirror that doesn’t reflect reality
  3. The spell book of Archmage Tugral (his rival)
  4. Tugral’s knife (which opens portals between planes)
  5. A three-sided mask

The party also dealt with a possessed crewmember (Jacosa) during a sea voyage, encountered a kraken (which Lucien calmed with song), and navigated the politics of Mistdam.

Arc 4: The Games and Brightspire (~Sessions 41-47)

The party attended the Isthmian Games at Vermillion and Azure, competing in individual and team melees, weightlifting, and a dramatic capture-the-flag battle involving a giant crab. Caesar secretly brokered a deal with the Kin to free slaves she encountered at the Games. Magnus won the individual melee but killed an opponent in controversial circumstances.

At Brightspire (the Congregation’s holy city), the party underwent spiritual trials. Elsbeth chose the path of light, burning her assassin blades and having her sight restored. Magnus, Lucien, and Aldara each confronted shadow versions of themselves on a bridge over an abyss, hearing whispers designed to exploit their deepest fears.

Arc 5: The Cloud-Capital (~Sessions 47-49)

The party explored the ruins of the Cloud-Capital, the fallen flying city of the Arcane Order. Aldara was formally elevated to Archmage by the ghostly remnants of the old mages’ council, inheriting the Arcane Order’s rights, debts, and obligations. The party recovered the artifacts Arianell sought and learned about his intentions.

At the Mirror of Heaven (a place in the desert where sand has fused to glass), Magnus broke his cursed sword’s hold on him in a gruelling trial under the desert sun.

Arc 6: The Worldshaper Cube (~Sessions 49-52)

The campaign climaxed in the ancient goblin vault, where the Worldshaper Cube — the “silver cube” first glimpsed early in the campaign — turned out to be a reality-editing device. Using Tugral’s knife to open a portal, the party entered the Cube’s interface: a dark void surrounded by memory nodes.

Each party member edited reality in turn while Arianell fought to maintain his existence. Lucien charmed the Lich. Aldara deleted the memories that had awakened him. Elsbeth reversed his recent changes. Magnus fought him in a mountaintop duel. Storr tried and failed to save his family. The Curator integrated with the Cube to assist.

Arianell was erased from reality — but his final words haunted the aftermath: “Very clever — but you will never know what else I changed…”

The Succession Crisis (Background Arc)

Running throughout the campaign, the Arrowmoor succession crisis saw Count Bertrand — backed by the Combine — murder Duchess Ethel and kidnap the heir Oswin. The party’s interventions, combined with the reality-editing at the campaign’s end, altered the timeline: Modwen, the Court Magician, later found herself studying memories of a war that never happened, uncertain what was real and what had been changed.

Consequences

The party’s use of the Worldshaper Cube left lasting marks on the world:

  • Temporal distortion: Widespread deja vu and unreliable memories. People forget key events. Dates shimmer and ink vanishes.
  • Enhanced magic: Magic becomes more powerful and unpredictable.
  • Weakened planar boundaries: Interplanar travel becomes more frequent. Visitors from other planes begin to appear (Lostan arrives at Brightspire “on a mission from god”).
  • Arianell’s echo: Cloud patterns, wind sounds, and folk tales keep his memory alive. He becomes a folk hero despite having been a villain.

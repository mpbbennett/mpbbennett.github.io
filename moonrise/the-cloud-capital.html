The Cloud-Capital

The Cloud-Capital was the flying city that served as the capital of the Arcane Order. It could be moved around the empire as required.

Construction

The city was constructed over many years, with new earthmotes added and connected by bridges to the existing structures. Four public teleportation circles existed in the city, each attended by a mage whose job was to deploy an antimagical field when passengers were embarking or disembarking to prevent teleporters landing on top of each other. At times of peak load, pre-booking was required.

Fall

After the Arcane Order was overthrown by the giants, Sky-of-Rain yoked her cloud giant castle to the Cloud-Capital. During the Clan Wars, rebels downed the entire city — a catastrophe that caused massive loss of life. The destruction of the Cloud-Capital was an important episode in galvanising opposition to giant rule.

Downed in an inaccessible location, it still lies in ruins today.

Archives

The Cloud-Capital’s archive vaults held the working papers of every Archmage of the Order. Among the surviving catalogues are the personal memoranda of Arianell, the Grandmaster condemned to elemental cessation around 250 years ago. Treasure-hunters with a serious interest in Arianell’s tomb tend to begin their research here.

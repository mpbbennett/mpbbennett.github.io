Dragonborn

Dragonborn are rare individuals who have devoted their lives to serving a dragon, and in return have been transformed with draconic features and longer lifespans.

Dragonborn Havens

Several dragonborn cities exist, ruled largely by the dragonborn themselves but always open to their allied dragons and committed to advancing their interests. Three havens are known:

  • Dryhaven — in the Rockfire Desert
  • Seahaven — in the Scalewind Isles
  • Icehaven — in the Icewyrm region

Dragonborn are also sent out into the world by their dragons on quests.

The Dragon Kingdom

One dragon and her dragonborn conquered a human kingdom and set themselves up as a despotic ruling caste. Other dragons look down on her actions. As the dragon grows old and weak, the dragonborn debate whether one of their own should succeed her or whether they should seek another dragon ruler.

Language

Dragonborn speak Draconic, in a clipped manner. Their names typically incorporate the name of their patron dragon.

Relations with Others

The Silver Dwarves of the Scalewind Isles have a healthy competitive respect for dragons, but hold the dragonborn in contempt — viewing their devotion as slavish.

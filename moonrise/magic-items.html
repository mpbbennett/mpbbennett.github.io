Magic Items

Homebrew magic items created for the Moonrise campaign. The campaign used Dungeon World mechanics, not D&D 5e.

First Mate’s Harpoon

2 Weight, Close, Reach, Near

A six-foot metal pole with jagged spiked teeth at one end and a loop at the other. It has a faint golden-yellow tinge to the spiked ends. The harpoon crackles with magical energy and smells faintly of ozone. Holding it with one hand is difficult — it shakes and jostles unless you are aiming to throw or gripping with both hands.

When you throw the Harpoon, roll +DEX. On a 10+, there is a peal of thunder, the air shakes, and the target is struck true — roll damage and you may pull the target towards you. On a 7-9, the usual complications apply, or the target pulls you to them instead.

Muting Maul

A long-shafted sledgehammer which, when swung at someone at full force, renders them completely silent for several minutes.

Me

(+1 damage)

I am a sword. Whenever anyone talks about me, they inadvertently refer to me in the first person. No way has been found yet to speak or write about me in the third person. Other than that, I’m just a damn fine sword.

Standard Items Used

The campaign also made use of several items from the standard rules: magic beans, a bag of devouring, a magic carpet, a cloak of the bat, a figurine of wondrous power, a folding boat, and goggles of darkvision.

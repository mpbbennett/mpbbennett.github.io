Mistdam

Mistdam is a major commercial centre of The League, built in an earthmote zone — a region where chunks of rock float naturally in the air due to elemental air influence. It controls the Wide-Awake Straits at the entrance to the Sea of Dreams.

The Earthmote Zone

The earthmote zone creates distinctive conditions: everyone within the zone naturally featherfalls, building towers and vertical structures is easier, and water rises from the sea forming constant mists that give the city its name. Vertical transport between levels — ground, earthmotes, and the connections between them — is a routine part of daily life.

Layout

Ground level is a massive port, perpetually shrouded in mist. The earthmotes above host the city’s most important buildings — temples, palaces, guild halls, and the homes of the wealthy. Living on an earthmote conveys status, escape from the fog, and better defences.

Government

Mistdam is ruled by a Grand Duke in title, but in practice governed by a council of executors — a mercantile oligarchy that has prospered under the League’s commercial freedoms.

Seer’s Castle

The site has been long settled, and one of the earthmotes — known as Seer’s Castle — contains ancient tombs predating the current city. It is rumoured to be haunted. Visitors are prohibited, but the university maintains active excavations. Deep within, sleeping beholders are said to lie undisturbed. Other tombs and ruins exist beneath the city at ground level.

Commerce

Mistdam’s merchants have mixed feelings about The Combine: they share the commercial mindset and trade extensively with them, but the Combine is also a competitive threat to the city’s mercantile interests.

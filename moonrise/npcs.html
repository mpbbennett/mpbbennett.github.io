The Antagonist

Arianell — A Lich, originally an Archmage of the old Arcane Order. He sought to use the Worldshaper Cube (an ancient goblin artifact hidden in a vault) to edit reality itself. The party ultimately reversed his changes, causing him to strobe in and out of existence. His last words: “Very clever — but you will never know what else I changed…”

In the aftermath, Arianell became a lingering presence — cloud patterns resembling him, wind sounding like his voice. He became a folk hero, and Lucien wrote ballads about him. His persecution by the Arcane Order is now a classic story of injustice.

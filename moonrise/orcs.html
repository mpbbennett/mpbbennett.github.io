Orcs

Orcs were brought to Moonrise as slaves by the Arcane Order and the Giants. They are widely regarded as brutish savages by other peoples, but amongst themselves have a rich culture of music, dance, and martial arts.

Current Situation

Many orcs are still slaves, especially around the Bay of Song. Others have been freed or escaped into the wilderness. A great slave revolt broke out among the orcs of the Isthmus during the fall of the Arcane Order, and the struggle for orc freedom continues.

History

The purchase of orcs as slaves was the work of one of the Arcane Order’s most notorious rulers, Gethin the Callous, who bought them from the Giants to work on great engineering projects. Gethin was eventually assassinated by a member of the Veil, possibly with the connivance of some archmagi.

Culture

Orcs who live freely maintain their own rich traditions. Their art forms centre on ballads, and they favour the Nehru suit in dress. Orcs speak Orcish, a language isolate, with names drawn from Hungarian conventions. The Bay of Song region, where many orcs live (whether free or enslaved), gives its name to their characteristic accent.

Half-Orcs

Half-orcs have mixed human and orc ancestry. They share the prejudice directed at orcs, but their dual heritage sometimes grants them greater freedom of movement in human-dominated societies.

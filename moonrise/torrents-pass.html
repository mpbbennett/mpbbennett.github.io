Torrent’s Pass

Torrent’s Pass is a city built next to a great waterfall where a major river flows over a significant escarpment. The city is constructed vertically into the cliff face, with higher-class neighbourhoods higher up and a slum known as Mistbottom at lagoon level.

Commerce

The city has long been a portage location for river trade, with canals built on either side to sheltered docks and a series of cranes for raising and lowering goods. Recently, an entrepreneur constructed a magical elevator to raise and lower entire ships by levitation. This has led to a boom in trade and a significant disruption to the city’s political economy.

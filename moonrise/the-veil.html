The Veil

Also known as: The Common Hand, the Voice of Mist (older names)
Values: Justice, Care
Symbol: A hand, yellow
Characteristic class: Monk

The Veil is a mysterious group responsible for assassinating powerful individuals. Their motto is “to make great men good and good men great” — but this is something of a euphemism. Their main activity is assassinating tyrants.

Organisation

The Veil is an ancient secret society operating in deep cover, taking a long view of history. Like The Kin, it operates a cellular structure so that few members are aware of the society’s true nature and extent. This also makes it difficult for the society to take decisive co-ordinated action.

The Veil originated as a heterodox Wayfinder movement and still retains religious elements. It operates secret temples and hideouts both in the wilderness and in city centres.

Recent Developments

Under new leadership, the movement has taken a “turn to the people,” seeking to influence popular movements in the city-states. This has been controversial with the older guard, who favour the traditional approach of targeted assassination.

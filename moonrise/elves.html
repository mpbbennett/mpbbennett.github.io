Elves

Elves have a reputation for moodiness, sudden flashes of insight, creativity, and maddening inconsistency. They are divided into two main groups.

Wood Elves (Sylvan Elves)

Wood elves live primarily in the Forest Kingdoms. They are deeply connected to the natural world — wood elves who immigrate to cities in other lands tend to lose their distinctive features within a couple of generations and integrate into half-elven or high-elven communities.

Wood elf culture is described in detail on the Forest Kingdoms page.

The Bowers

Each elven bower (city) has a distinctive character:

  • Rose Bower — built around a massive tree, as tall as a great tower, amid a forest of large but normal trees. Seat of the High King.
  • Orchid — a treetop city built over a swamp.
  • Lilac — a mushroom city.
  • Lily — ground-level streets with all buildings built in and around trees.
  • Jasmine — a city of woven grass.
  • Lotus — the Lotus elves live on boats on the upper great lake and the rivers feeding it. They alone cultivate the trees strong enough to withstand the acidic waters. The lake can be navigated by other boats, but acid damage makes for very short lifespans for non-elven vessels.
  • Sage — a cactus city in the desert.

Wood elven architecture draws on North American indigenous traditions.

Wood Elf Customs

Wood elf druids carry a variety of jungle frogs they have befriended. These frogs produce hallucinogenic substances imbibed by licking them — the traditional wood elven intoxicant is ayahuasca. The characteristic art form of the wood elves is dance in the Kathakali tradition, and gladiatorial combat is a major entertainment. Wood elves drink tea and eat in a Thai-influenced cuisine. Their dress follows Chinese conventions.

Wood elves are also known for trial by combat, which prevents their aristocratic politics — as vicious as anywhere else’s — from frequently breaking down into open war.

The Forest Secret

The forests of the wood elves are home to regular incursions by aberrations from the underdark. That the forests are always in danger of such corruption is a secret the elves keep from other races. It helps to explain why the wood elves are both so martially skilled and yet so slow to expand their territory — elven warriors are regularly needed against aberrant threats.

Language

Wood elves speak Sylvan, part of the Elven/Sylvan language family. Sylvan names follow Italian conventions, in the form “[Name] of the [House]” (e.g. “Corrin of the Tall White Lily”). High elves speak High Elven, also part of this family, with names drawn from Latin tradition in the form “X of [location]”.

The names of elven cities need no translation in other languages, because the words for flowers all derive from the Elven.

Creation Myth

The elven creation myth involves the elves germinating from plants.

High Elves (Sundered Elves)

High elves are a diaspora spread across the continent following the collapse of the High Elven Empire roughly 2,000 years ago. They are known for arrogance and erudition, and are prominent in The Congregation.

The Last High Elven City

One remaining city of high elves exists in Moonrise. It largely escaped the cataclysm due to its remoteness and the refusal of its civic leaders to send their garrison back to the homeland during the disaster. The city has not played a significant role in politics since then, being small and focused on its own defence, though it has remained a centre of scholarship.

The city is rife with a drug called Black Milk, distilled from Black Lotus flowers grown in the jungle. The soporific drug produces intense hallucinatory dreams, often apparently oracular. The city has been gradually selling off its ancient treasures to pay for its addiction — treasures sufficient that this has been sustainable for a long time.

In recent years the supply of Black Milk has increased, leaving the city more sleepy and disconnected than ever. The truth is that a vampire has been funnelling more of the drug into the city, using the dreams of the elves to power a dark ritual to bring back the Bleeding King.

The city is the only place in Moonrise with a functional temple of the old High Elven god.

The Slavers’ City

There is a city of slavers based on an island, predominantly high elven. It was a former high elven stronghold. The Bleeding King was a major customer, and the city has experienced an economic downturn since his fall.

Drow

The Drow are a subterranean elven people, somehow connected to the Cataclysm that destroyed the High Elven Empire. They speak Drow, a language in the Elven/Sylvan family, and their names follow Romanian conventions. The High Elven Empire fought wars against the Drow before its fall.

Elves and the Feywild

Elves (and gnomes) have a natural connection to the Feywild and are sometimes thought to have originated there and migrated to the material world.

Elves and Dwarves

The War of the Beard was a vicious conflict between the Greenspire Dwarves and the wood elves, fought between the Cataclysm and the Goblin Despotate. It began as a local feud over tree-cutting and escalated into a great war. The costly conflict ended in stalemate, and since then leaders have kept the two peoples separate. A great deal of cultural mistrust remains.

Language Families

Languages in Moonrise are grouped into mutually intelligible families:

  • Common family: Standard, Arrowmoor, Lakelandic, Central, Northern, Bay, Old Common, Halfling, and local dialects. All mutually intelligible to varying degrees.
  • Elven/Sylvan family: High Elven, Sylvan, Drow. Share a writing system.
  • Dwarven family: Gold Dwarven and Silver Dwarven. Share a writing system.
  • Draconic: Northern and Southern dialects. Used by dragons and dragonborn. Dwarven script is sometimes used for Draconic.
  • Goblish: A language isolate.
  • Giant: Storm, Stone, Hill, Frost, and Fire Giant dialects. Related to the Reavers’ Tongue.
  • Orcish: A language isolate.
  • Primordial: Infernal (fire), Terran (earth), and other elemental dialects.

Profession-Based Surnames

In League and Arrowmoor culture, surnames often derive from occupations. The name generators organise these across 24 profession categories (Angler, Scryer, Cartwright, etc.) and 16 real-world linguistic traditions.

Elven and Dwarven Place Names

The names of elven cities need no translation in other languages, because the words for flowers all derive from the Elven. Similarly, the words for gemstones all derive from the Dwarven.

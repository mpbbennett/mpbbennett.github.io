The Three

Also known as: The Living Gods
Values: Authority, Care
Symbol: Three dots, white on blue
Characteristic class: Paladin

While most religion in Moonrise is a matter of faith, the Three directly manifest themselves as beings of godlike power in this world. They rule a growing empire and attract followers far beyond its borders.

History

xx years ago, the Three arrived out of the blue in the region now known as the Triland. At the time, this was the realm of the Bleeding King, a vampire lord who had ruled for more than two centuries and built up an extensive vampiric aristocracy who fed on the blood of the populace. The Three slew the King and claimed the kingdom for their own.

After the exceptional cruelty of vampiric rule, the coming of the Three was greeted with wild enthusiasm.

The Three are known individually as xx — Child/Adult/Elder or Recalled/Immediate/Ensuing? Ask Michael. The three major cities of the kingdom have been renamed accordingly.

Government

Compared to neighbouring realms, the government of the Triland is unusually uncorrupted and oriented towards the wellbeing of the populace. Having removed the vampiric aristocracy, the Three have taken steps to ensure the social structure remains unusually flat, and living standards for peasants are higher here than in any other major realm.

Within the empire’s borders, all other religions and foreign organisations that might divide the loyalties of subjects are banned — the Old Gods, the Congregation, the Combine (although some Combine joint ventures with the government are allowed). The hunt for remaining vampires created a pretext for the construction of an extensive police apparatus.

The Slayers

The Three’s fight against the Bleeding King inspired an order known as the Slayers, who seek to continue the work of wiping out the remaining vampire aristocracy in the Lakes region. The Slayers have also expanded beyond the empire, countering other undead and evil threats besides vampires.

Foreign Relations

The Three attract a growing number of adherents outside their borders. In neighbouring countries, followers are often viewed as fifth-columnists. Further afield, where there is no particular prospect of invasion, the faith is viewed more like any other.

The Secret of the Three

The Three were in reality an adventuring party of unrelated humans who acquired immense powers from an intensely magical meteorite whose origin even they do not understand. The “Mother” (as she was originally known) was an agent of the Veil who had been tasked with destroying the Bleeding King by any means necessary.

The Three are engaged in tracking down and eliminating anyone who might have knowledge of their prior lives, and in trying to learn more about the true source of their power.

Goals

Long-term: World peace through world domination.
Proximate: Eliminate all evidence of the Three’s earlier lives. Understand the source of their power. Expand the empire. Gain followers outside the empire. Defeat evils to gain popular support.

Girih Pattern Exploration Lab

Variations on Islamic geometric decoration — colour, geometry, density, animation

I. Star Geometry Variations

6-Pointed Star (Hexagonal)

Star of David / hexagonal tiling. Simplest girih-family motif. Dense, honeycomb feel.

8-Pointed Star (Octagonal)

Classic Islamic pattern found in the Alhambra. Square-compatible lattice with rich interlocking.

10-Pointed Star (Current Design)

The existing girih pattern. Decagonal symmetry creates complex overlapping regions.

12-Pointed Star (Dodecagonal)

Maximum complexity. Found in Mamluk and Ottoman architecture. Very dense strapwork.

II. Colour Palettes

Gold on Black (Original)

The current palette. Warm, scholarly, evocative of illuminated manuscripts.

Iznik Blue & White

Inspired by Ottoman Iznik ceramics. Cool, precise, mathematical feeling.

Emerald & Jade

Deep green palette evoking Mughal architecture. Rich and contemplative.

Copper & Rose

Warm copper tones with rose highlights. Persian metalwork aesthetic.

Midnight & Silver

Cool silver on deep navy. Celestial, astronomical instrument feeling.

Terracotta & Ochre

Earthy North African palette. Warm, grounded, archaeological.

III. Tiling & Density

Sparse / Breathing

Large cells, thin lines, lots of negative space. Minimal and elegant.

Ultra-Dense Micropattern

Tiny cells packed tight. Reads as texture from a distance, reveals structure up close.

Edge Fade (Border Mode)

Pattern fades from left edge toward centre. The actual border rendering strategy.

Radial Fade

Pattern concentrated in centre, fading outward. Medallion/rosette effect.

IV. Interlacing & Strapwork

Woven Strapwork

Lines weave over and under each other with shadow. True girih interlacing effect.

Neon Glow

Luminous bloom effect on the lines. Modern/futuristic take on traditional geometry.

V. Filled Tiles (Zellige-Inspired)

Zellige Colour Fill

Filled polygonal regions with varied colour. Inspired by Moroccan zellige tilework.

Monochrome Zellige

Subtle tonal variations in a single hue family. Sophisticated, understated.

VI. Animation & Motion

Breathing Opacity

Lines gently pulse in and out. Subtle, organic movement.

Wave Propagation

Brightness ripples outward from centre. Creates sense of depth and life.

Slow Rotation

Individual motifs rotate imperceptibly. Hypnotic, kaleidoscopic.

Progressive Draw

Pattern draws itself line by line. Reveals the construction process.

VII. Layered Geometry

Dual Stars (8 + 12)

Two star tilings overlaid at different scales and colours. Complex moire interference.

Nested Rosettes

Concentric star motifs at decreasing scale within each cell. Fractal-like depth.

Hexagonal Lattice

True hexagonal tiling with 6-fold symmetry. Cells perfectly tessellate without gaps.

Quasi-Penrose (5-fold)

Aperiodic tiling inspired by Penrose patterns. Never repeats but maintains 5-fold symmetry.

VIII. Colour Experiments

Spectral Gradient

Hue shifts smoothly across the canvas. Each line coloured by its position.

Duotone: Teal & Gold

Two-colour system where alternating motif layers use contrasting hues.

Ember Glow

Deep reds through orange to bright yellow. Molten metal aesthetic.

Frost Crystal

Pale cyan and white on deep blue. Crystalline, fragile, mathematical.

IX. Border Simulations

Border: Gold (Current)

The existing site border simulation. Gold girih fading from edge.

Border: Iznik Blue

Cool blue variant for the side borders. More academic, less manuscript.

Border: Gradient Shift

Colour shifts from gold at top to teal at bottom along the border.

Border: Soft Glow

Neon-bloom version of the border. Lines glow against the dark background.
